# Dismissal Enhanced
Ever wanted to dismiss your bro, but wanted different pricing? This mod lets you adjust dismissal compensation from the option menu.

## Price Formula Options

These options directly affect the final dismissal compensation price.

### General

- `Minimum Compensation Floor`: The final compensation can never go below this amount.

### Hire Compensation

- `Hire Cost Percent`: Adds a percentage of the brother's hiring cost to compensation.
- `Enable Days With Company`: When enabled, days with the company add compensation.
- `Gold Per Day`: Flat gold added for each day with the company when days compensation is enabled.
- `Enable Level Bonus`: When enabled, compensation gains a flat bonus based on the brother's level bracket.
- `Level 1-5 Bonus`: Flat gold added for brothers from level 1 to 5.
- `Level 6-10 Bonus`: Flat gold added for brothers from level 6 to 10.
- `Level 11+ Bonus`: Flat gold added for brothers from level 11 upward.

### Equipment Deduction

- `Enable Equipment Deduction`: When enabled, counted equipped items reduce final compensation.
- `Use Sell Price`: When enabled, equipment deduction uses sell price. When disabled, it uses base item value.
- `Equipment Value Percent`: Percent of counted equipment value deducted from compensation.
- `Count Head Armor`: Includes equipped head armor in the equipment deduction.
- `Count Body Armor`: Includes equipped body armor in the equipment deduction.
- `Count Mainhand`: Includes the equipped mainhand item in the equipment deduction.
- `Count Offhand`: Includes the equipped offhand item in the equipment deduction.
- `Count Accessory`: Includes the equipped accessory in the equipment deduction.
- `Count Ammo`: Includes the equipped ammo item in the equipment deduction.

### Injury Compensation

- `Permanent Injury Gold`: Flat gold added for each permanent injury.
- `Temporary Injury Gold`: Flat gold added for each temporary injury.
